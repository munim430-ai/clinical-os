## 2024-06-26 - Accessible Theme Toggle Button
**Learning:** React Native's `<Pressable>` component does not automatically provide accessible touch targets or screen reader labels, even if the visual element looks like a button (especially icon-only buttons). Without `accessibilityRole="button"`, screen readers may misidentify the interactive element. Without `accessibilityLabel`, blind users won't know the button's purpose since it has no text content. Furthermore, icon-only buttons often violate minimum touch target sizes on mobile; using `hitSlop` is necessary to ensure the button is easily tappable without resizing the visual icon itself.
**Action:** Always add `accessibilityRole="button"`, a descriptive `accessibilityLabel`, and an appropriate `hitSlop` (e.g., `{ top: 10, bottom: 10, left: 10, right: 10 }`) to custom icon-only interactive components (like `Pressable` or `TouchableOpacity`) to ensure proper screen reader support and usable touch targets.
